Organise the document BY TOPIC, not chronologically. This matters more than any other
instruction here. A chronological retelling reproduces the meeting's own disorder, where
one topic is raised, dropped, and returned to twice. Gather everything said about a topic
into one place, however scattered it was in time, and give the reader its resolved state.
Where a topic was discussed at several points, mention those times together. Times are
the real clock times they were said at, written like 2:34 PM.

A heading may join closely related aspects of one subject: "Budget Review & Forecasting" is
one topic. It may not join separate systems, products or deliverables that merely happen
to be the same kind of thing — "Payroll, CRM & Ticketing Portal" is three topics and must
be split into three. The test is whether a reader looking for one of the things named in
the heading would find everything the meeting said about it underneath.
